- The gap stays `0.28` radians, the value the old private maths used, and it stays centred on each 30° boundary. January's boundary is 12 o'clock. The painted arc is inset by half the gap on each side, so cell k ends one full gap before cell k+1 starts, and the midpoint tick sits on the painted arc. - Labels sit on the month mark, `(calendarMonth − 1) × 30°`, so January is exactly at 12 o'clock and `dialTurnDegrees` brings that mark to the top (`-(m − 1) × 30` in `ltr`, the opposite sign in `rtl`).
