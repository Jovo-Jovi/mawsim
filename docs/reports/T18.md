### Notes for the reviewer

- The gap stays `0.28` radians, the value the old private maths used, and it stays centred on each 30° boundary. January's boundary is 12 o'clock. The painted arc is inset by half the gap on each side, so cell k ends one full gap before cell k+1 starts, and the midpoint tick sits on the painted arc. A gap placed entirely after the arc would put that tick in the empty sector.
