### Design specification

- States: `guide-ar` · `guide-en` · `home-ar` · `card-hover-en` · `card-focus-ar`
- Criteria: `UA-01` · `UA-02` · `UA-03` · `UA-04` · `UA-05` · `UA-06` · `UA-07` · `UA-08` · `UA-09` · `UA-10` · `UA-11` · `UA-12` · `UA-13` · `UA-14` · `UA-19`

The card stops being a 4:3 photo with a ring badge on it. It becomes the year dial in small: a square frame, the
`SeasonRing` at the frame's edge, and the photo as a circle inside the ring. Nothing is drawn over the photo.

1. **Frame.** `.frame` is square (`aspect-ratio: 1`) at the card's full inline size. The `SeasonRing` fills it, and its
   outer stroke edge meets the frame's edge. The photo circle is centred. Its edge sits `--ring-inset` inside the
   ring's inner stroke edge, so a band of card surface shows between ring and photo. The circles are round
   (`--radius-pill`), clip with `overflow: hidden` and have a 1px `--color-hairline` border (§3). The ring sits under
   the photo circle in paint order, or has `pointer-events: none`.
2. **Ring.** Twelve cells from 12 o'clock. In-season cells are lit in the kind colour and the others are track with the
   hairline. This month's accent tick spans the band only, from its inner stroke edge outward, and never enters the
   photo circle. The ring runs clockwise in `en` and is mirrored in `ar` (the existing `scaleX(-1)` is allowed on a
   ring with no text, §13). It is `aria-hidden`. The card's season sentence stays as its text equivalent (UA-13).
3. **Photo.** `next/image`, `object-fit: cover`, `object-position: center`, lazy loading, `sizes` `(min-width: 64rem)
   20vw, 40vw`, and alt text in the page's locale. **Fallback** (no manifest row): the kind-colour disc in the same
   circle, with no inner ring and no halo of its own. The name and counter-name sit on their `--color-surface` label
   (the existing `.copy`), never on the kind fill (§2). Only the `card` slot changes. The `page` and `hero` fallbacks
   keep their layout, and their `SeasonRing` picks up the new geometry.
4. **Hover and focus** (§7): the card lifts with `--shadow-2`. The photo (or disc) zooms to `--zoom-photo` inside its
   circle, which clips it. The card tilts `--tilt-card`, with the sign flipped in `rtl`. The ring glows in the kind
   colour to `--glow-ring-opacity`, as a circular halo centred on the ring that the card's own box contains. Only
   `transform` and `opacity` animate. The focus outline stays 2px with a 2px offset.
5. **Reduced motion:** no zoom, tilt, glow or transition. Shadow and focus ring only.
6. **Copy block** below the frame, unchanged: name, counter-name, kind label, season sentence. The whole card is one link.

`SeasonRing.tsx`, `SeasonRing.module.css` and `SeasonRing.test.tsx`; `src/modules/produce/index.ts`. Proves

1. Add every version 3 token of `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` §2 with its exact value. `--dial-size`, `--dial-picker-size` and
   `--arc-size` take their `40rem` and `64rem` values in the existing breakpoint blocks. The rest are `--dial-step`,
   `--ring-inset`, `--story-step`, `--story-scrub`, `--story-back-scale`, `--story-back-opacity`, `--dur-flip`,
   `--flip-stagger` and `--zoom-scroll`. Keep `--scene-turn` and `--scene-size`, which T21 removes.
2. Write `dial-geometry.ts`, pure TypeScript with no React import and the one source of ring maths. It exports the
   viewBox size and the ring's stroke radius, band width and inner and outer edge radii, all in viewBox units. It also
   exports functions with qualified names (`GLOSSARY.md` §3: `CalendarMonth`, never a bare `month` type) for:
   - the path of cell k (starting at 12 o'clock, `--dial-step` per cell, with a small gap);
   - the accent tick of a `CalendarMonth`, spanning the band;
   - the label point of a `CalendarMonth` on a given radius, for `ltr` or `rtl` (counter-clockwise in `rtl`);
   - the dial's turn in degrees for a selected `CalendarMonth` and direction: `-(m − 1) × 30` in `ltr`, the opposite
     sign in `rtl`.

   Export the module through `index.ts`.
3. `SeasonRing` draws its cells and tick from the module, with the same props. Its stroke moves outward so the band's
   outer edge meets the viewBox edge. The band width equals the stylesheet's widest `stroke-width`. Delete the
   component's private maths.
4. Tests, each named for the break it catches. In `dial-geometry.test.ts`:
   - 12 cells of 30°; January starts at 12 o'clock; cell k ends where k+1 starts, less the gap;
   - the tick at the month's midpoint, inside the band;
   - the `rtl` label points mirror the `ltr` ones (x' = size − x, y equal);
   - the turn for months 1, 4 and 12 in both directions;
   - the outer edge equals half the viewBox;
   - the widest `stroke-width` in `SeasonRing.module.css` equals the band width.

   `SeasonRing.test.tsx` keeps every assertion's meaning; its tick coordinates are recomputed from the module, and the
