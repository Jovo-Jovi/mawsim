### Design specification

- States: `guide-ar` · `guide-en` · `guide-chip-focus-en`
- Criteria: `UA-01` · `UA-02` · `UA-03` · `UA-04` · `UA-05` · `UA-06` · `UA-07` · `UA-10` · `UA-12` · `UA-14` · `UA-15`

Two motions on the guide, both once, both `transform` and `opacity` only.

1. **Reveal on scroll.** `Reveal` (`src/modules/guide/Reveal.tsx`, `"use client"`, the product's first one in a module, no
   dependency) wraps the card grid and the sample-data notice in `page.tsx`. It renders one `div` with `display: contents`
   (no layout change) and, in one `useEffect` with **no dependency array** (it re-runs when a chip or month link brings new
   children, and is idempotent), calls a pure helper in `reveal-items.ts` that takes the root, the window's height, a
   reduced-motion flag and an observer factory, so the logic is testable in the node environment (`vitest.config.ts`
   has no DOM and none may be added). The helper finds the descendants carrying `data-reveal-item` (set on each `li` cell in
   `CardGrid.tsx` and on the notice's wrapper in `page.tsx`). An item whose top is already inside the first viewport is
   left alone. Every other item gets `data-reveal="pending"` and is observed by one `IntersectionObserver`; when it
   enters the view it becomes `data-reveal="shown"`, is unobserved and is never made pending again. The component sets no
   state, so the server HTML equals the first client render and nothing is hidden before the script runs: **without
   JavaScript, without `IntersectionObserver`, and under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the effect hides nothing.** The
   effect is safe when it runs twice (a `shown` item is skipped, a `pending` one is observed again; cleanup disconnects).
2. **Reveal styling** in `Reveal.module.css`, under the local `.root`: `[data-reveal="pending"]` is `opacity: 0` and
   `transform: translateY(var(--reveal-rise))`; `[data-reveal="shown"]` returns to `opacity: 1` and `transform: none`
   through a transition of exactly those two properties over `var(--dur-reveal)` with `var(--ease-out)`. Stagger is a
   transition delay of `var(--reveal-stagger)` times the item's position among its siblings modulo four (`:nth-child(4n+1)`
   to `4n`: at most three steps, a deliberate narrowing of §7's "at most 8 in a row"; only cards and the notice reveal, not
   the header band, which is always in the first viewport). The grid's existing load-time `rise` animation (first-viewport
   cards) stays; a pending or shown item must have `animation: none` and win over `.cell:nth-child(n)` whatever the
   stylesheet order (raise specificity; do not use `!important` or `:global`). Reduced motion: no hiding and no transition.
3. **Selected pill.** On arrival the selected month link's `.label` and the selected chip grow in from
   `var(--pill-grow-from)` to scale 1 with an opacity fade, `var(--dur-2)`, `var(--ease-out)`, once, with the keyframes
   named `grow` (`animation-iteration-count: 1`, `animation-fill-mode: backwards`), keyed to `[aria-current="true"]` so that
   a new selection plays it. The existing `background-color` and colour transitions stay. Add one token to `tokens.css`:
   `--pill-grow-from: 0.92` (the scale the design system names in §7); no other token, and one row for it in the
   `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` §2 Motion table. Reduced motion: no animation. The reduced-motion rule must repeat the
   `[aria-current="true"]` selectors (`.chip[aria-current="true"]`, `.link[aria-current="true"] .label`) so it beats the
   arrival rule on specificity; a `.chip { animation: none }` alone loses.
4. **Focus and size.** Nothing here changes a target size (`--target-min`), the focus ring, markup order or any string.
   The chip's ring stays visible while its pill grows (capture `guide-chip-focus-en`). Layout shift stays 0.
5. **Captures.** Settled captures are full-page and do not scroll, so below-the-fold cells and the notice appear
   **pending** (hidden) in them; the reduced-motion twins show every item. Say so in the report, and let the e2e prove the
   final state. Do not change the capture scripts and do not reveal on `load` to hide this.
