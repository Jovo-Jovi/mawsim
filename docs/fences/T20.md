### Design specification

- States: `guide-ar` · `guide-en` · `guide-sep-en`
- Criteria: `UA-01` · `UA-02` · `UA-03` · `UA-04` · `UA-05` · `UA-06` · `UA-07` · `UA-08` · `UA-09` · `UA-10` · `UA-11` · `UA-12` · `UA-14`

Every guide control is a link. (Whether the guide's content shows with no script at all is an open carry-forward of
`T20-a1.md`, outside this task's Paths.) This task adds movement to a month change and nothing else: the dial
turns to the new month and the cards re-sort. Nothing plays on first load, nothing loops, nothing moves once the change
has finished (`DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` §7). With reduced motion or without JavaScript the page is the settled page. The three
captures are settled pages. The turn itself is proven by `e2e/guide-turn.spec.ts`'s sampled angles, not by a capture.

1. **Dependency (PR-47, OD-11 §5).** `gsap` core plus `gsap/Flip`, GreenSock's Standard "no charge" licence. It is the
   only `package.json` change (one `dependencies` line, already on the branch). No `@gsap/react`, ScrollTrigger, Lenis,
   jsdom or other package.
2. **`CardGrid` keeps every card** (built): ten `li` in catalogue order with `data-flip-id`. A card outside the selected
   set is `hidden` and has no `data-reveal-item`. No `key` on `CardGrid`. The empty set keeps today's copy and links.
3. **`dial-turn.ts`** (built): the short-way offset, derived from `dialTurnDegrees`, in (−180, 180], with the sign
   mirrored in `rtl`.
4. **`GuideMotion`** (built): the one client component. It records `Flip.getState` on a plain primary click of a picker
   link and never calls `preventDefault`. After a month change, inside `gsap.context` + `gsap.matchMedia`
   (`no-preference` only), the `[data-dial-turn]` wrapper turns from the offset to 0 over `--dur-3` and each
   `[data-dial-label]` counter-rotates. `Flip.from` re-sorts the cards over `--dur-flip`, with the rise and the stagger
   read from tokens. Only `rotation`, `x`, `y`, `scale` and `opacity` move. **Every inline style the component writes,
   `animation` included, is written through GSAP inside the context or removed in the effect's clean-up**, so a revert
   at any moment leaves no inline `transform`, `opacity`, `will-change` or `animation`.
5. **`MonthPicker`** keeps the `href` and the default prefetch of every link. Its only changes are `data-month-step` on
   the two step links and the pill fix of step 2.
