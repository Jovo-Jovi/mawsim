### Design specification

- States: `guide-ar` · `guide-en` · `guide-turn-en`
- Criteria: `UA-01` · `UA-02` · `UA-03` · `UA-04` · `UA-05` · `UA-06` · `UA-07` · `UA-08` · `UA-09` · `UA-10` · `UA-11` · `UA-12` · `UA-14`

The guide already works as links with no script. This task adds movement to a month change and nothing else: the dial
turns to the new month and the cards re-sort. Nothing plays on first load, nothing loops, nothing moves after the
visitor stops (`DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` §7). With reduced motion or without JavaScript the page is the settled page.

1. **Dependency (PR-47, OD-11 §5).** Fence line: `gsap`, GreenSock's Standard "no charge" licence, core plus the
   `gsap/Flip` plugin, for the guide's dial turn and card re-sort (OD-11 §3, `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` §7). It is the only
   change to `package.json` (one `dependencies` line) and the lockfile follows. `@gsap/react`, ScrollTrigger, Lenis and
   every other plugin stay out. `Flip` is registered once, at module scope of the one client file below.
2. **`CardGrid` keeps every card.** It renders all ten produce cards in catalogue order, each `li` carrying
   `data-flip-id={slug}`. A card outside the selected set (month and kind) carries `hidden`; a card inside it carries
   `data-reveal-item`, a hidden one does not, so `Reveal` is unchanged. The visible cards keep today's order and
   layout (verify that `inSeasonIn` order is catalogue order). The `key` on `CardGrid` in `page.tsx` is removed, since
   a remount would defeat Flip. An empty set still renders the empty-state copy and links of today, and the page keeps
   `view=chart` working as in T19. Without JavaScript the page is the same complete page.
3. **`dial-turn.ts`** (pure, `src/modules/guide/`, no React): the dial's offset between two selected months for a
   direction, the short way round, in degrees, derived from `dialTurnDegrees` (the one source of angles). The offset
   is the previous turn minus the new turn, normalised into (−180, 180]; so December → January is a 30° step, not
   330°, and the sign mirrors in `rtl`.
4. **`GuideMotion`** (`src/modules/guide/GuideMotion.tsx`, `"use client"`, exported): the one client component of this
   task. It wraps the page's header and `Reveal` in one `div` with `display: contents` (CSS module, no layout effect).
   It receives `selectedMonth`, `direction` and the server-rendered children. It remembers the selected month in a ref,
   so the first render animates nothing.
   - On a plain primary click on one of the picker's own links (the twelve months and previous and next; add a stable
     hook to `MonthPicker` for the two step links), it records `Flip.getState` of the visible cards by
     `data-flip-id`. It never calls `preventDefault`; the navigation is the link's. Modified clicks are ignored.
   - After the commit in which `selectedMonth` changed, inside `gsap.matchMedia` with
     `(prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)` only: the dial turns from the offset of `dial-turn.ts` to 0 over
     `--dur-3`, eased as `--ease-in-out`; the cards re-sort with `Flip.from` over `--dur-flip`: a card that stays slides
     to its new place, a card that leaves fades out and is hidden again at the end, a new card rises by
     `--reveal-rise` and fades in, staggered by `--flip-stagger` for at most the first eight. A kind, view or language
     change without a month change animates nothing.
   - **The dial.** Add hooks to `YearDial` (`data-dial-turn` on `.turn`; a wrapper element around each label's content
     carrying `data-dial-label`) so GSAP rotates an element that has no transform of its own. The `.turn` wrapper
     rotates by the offset to 0, and each label wrapper counter-rotates by the negative of it, so no label is ever
     tilted, flipped or mirrored, and the selected link's `grow` keyframes are untouched. `YearDial` stays server-rendered
     and free of `"use client"`; the markup changes only by these hooks.
   - **Tokens, not numbers.** Durations, the stagger and the rise are read from the custom properties with
     `getComputedStyle` (`--dur-3`, `--dur-flip`, `--flip-stagger`, `--reveal-rise`). GSAP core cannot parse
     `cubic-bezier`, so name its core ease with a comment saying which token it stands for. No colour, `px` or raw time
     literal in the file. Only `rotation`, `x`, `y`, `scale` (Flip's own) and `opacity` move.
   - **Clean-up.** The whole animation lives in one `gsap.context`/`matchMedia` created in an effect and reverted in its
     clean-up, so an unmount (Strict Mode, or leaving the guide) leaves no inline `transform`, `opacity`, `will-change`
     or listener behind. The component reads the scroll position, the viewport and `prefers-reduced-motion` only. It
     sends, stores and fetches nothing.
5. **Page.** `page.tsx` wraps its content in `GuideMotion` and passes `selectedMonth` and the direction from
   `directionOf(locale)`. Its markup, the `h1` copy and every link are otherwise unchanged. No string moves into the
   component, and no catalogue key is added.
