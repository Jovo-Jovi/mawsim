### Design specification

- States: `home-ar` · `home-en` · `skip-focus-ar`
- Criteria: `UA-01` · `UA-02` · `UA-03` · `UA-04` · `UA-05` · `UA-06` · `UA-07` · `UA-10` · `UA-11` · `UA-14` · `UA-15` · `UA-18` · `UA-20`

The shell is the frame of every later page. Arabic is the primary composition: in `home-ar` the wordmark sits at the inline start
(right) and the language link at the inline end, in IBM Plex Sans Arabic with its own size and leading. `home-en` is the
LTR adaptation in Inter, not a mirrored copy. Header: sticky glass (`--color-surface-glass` with
`backdrop-filter: blur(var(--blur-glass))`, and solid `--color-surface` under `@supports not (backdrop-filter: …)`), a
`--color-hairline` bottom rule, and the container (`--container`, gutters per §2). Inside it:
- `Wordmark`: the page language's `app.name` large, and the other catalogue's `app.name` beneath it as the counter-name in
  `--color-ink-muted`, with its own `lang`.
- `nav` (with an `aria-label`): only the Home link for now, with `aria-current="page"` and a primary-tint pill on the home page.
- `LanguageLink`.

Main: the one `h1` (`app.tagline`) at `--text-display`, with tight leading and tracking in `en` only. Footer:
`SampleDataNotice` (a quiet band in `--color-track` with the `notice` glyph), the "collects nothing" line and the
`LanguageLink`. Both themes come from `prefers-color-scheme` (§4). The skip link is the first focusable element. It is visually hidden until
focused and then shows in `--color-primary` with the focus ring. Every target is at least `--target-min`. No motion beyond
the link colour transition (`--dur-1`), and none under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Not captured: the not-found page. The runner counts its 404
as a failed request, so the probe proves it (Done-when 3) and the reviewer reads its code.

### Stage 2 — Tokens, fonts and the shell components

Done-when 1, 7–10 and runs every Done-when command.

5. **Tokens and fonts.**

     layout and motion) with the `ar` overrides of the type rows under `:root:lang(ar)`, plus `color-scheme: light dark`. It adds two tokens
