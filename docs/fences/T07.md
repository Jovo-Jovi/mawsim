### Design specification

- States: `guide-ar` · `guide-en` · `guide-fruit-ar` · `guide-empty-en` · `guide-chip-focus-en`
- Criteria: `UA-01` · `UA-02` · `UA-03` · `UA-04` · `UA-05` · `UA-06` · `UA-07` · `UA-08` · `UA-09` · `UA-10` · `UA-11` · `UA-13` · `UA-14` · `UA-15` · `UA-16` · `UA-17` · `UA-18` · `UA-19`

The guide answers one question at a market stall: what is good this month. Arabic is the primary composition (`guide-ar` is judged
first); `guide-en` is its LTR adaptation, not a mirror. Inside the 72rem container, top to bottom: the one `h1` (`guide.title` with the
shown month's catalogue name), the `KindChips`, the `CardGrid`, the `SampleDataNotice`. No hero, intro paragraph, count or extra badge.

**KindChips** — a `nav` labelled `guide.kinds` holding a list of four links from the inline start: `guide.all`, `kind.fruit`,
`kind.vegetable`, `kind.leafy`. Each link is at least `--target-min` in both dimensions, with `--radius-pill`. The active link carries
`aria-current="true"` and the one filled pill (`--color-primary` ground, `--color-on-primary` text). The others are `--color-primary`
text on the ground with no box, so the pill alone marks the choice. Hover changes colour or underline only. `focus-visible` shows the 2px
`--color-primary` ring with a 2px offset (§9). At 390 the chips wrap; they never scroll sideways (UA-15).

**CardGrid** — a `<ul>` of `ProduceCard`s in `content/produce.json` order: 2 columns below 40rem, 3 from 40rem, 4 from 64rem, gaps
from `--space-*`. The empty state (UA-16) is one sentence, `guide.empty`, with the shown month's name. Beneath it are two links:
`guide.showAll` (same month, all kinds) and `guide.showThisMonth` (same kind, `month` dropped). The second link shows only when the
shown month is not this month.

**ProduceCard** (§6) — one link to `/{locale}/produce/{slug}` on a `--color-surface` card with a `--color-hairline` border and
`--radius-lg`. Inside it, in order:
- the `ProducePhoto` (`slot="card"`, not `priority`, `currentMonth` = this month);
- the name as an `h2` in `<bdi>`;
- the counter-name in the other locale, with its own `lang` and `<bdi>`, in `--color-ink-muted`;
- the kind label;
- the season sentence (`seasonSentence`, the ring's text equivalent, UA-13).

With a photograph, the `SeasonRing` sits as a badge in the frame's inline-end corner, its size reserved. With the fallback tile there is
no badge, because the tile already carries the ring. The frame's wrapper is then `aria-hidden="true"`, because the card's own text
repeats the names. States:
- hover: `--shadow-2`, no lift;
- `focus-visible`: the 2px primary ring with a 2px offset;
- active: no lift.

This page has no animation and no transition; T11 adds the lift and every transition.

**Shell** — `SiteHeader` gains a `nav.guide` link after Home, carrying `aria-current="page"` on the guide. Both language links keep the
guide's query. `NotFoundCopy` gains a `notFound.guide` link to the guide (§6 `NotFound`). This lands the T02 review's deferred
header/not-found links (`docs/reviews/T02-a1.md:56`).

Both themes: every text pair passes `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` §2 contrast. `--color-on-primary` on `--color-primary` must be ≥ 7:1.
