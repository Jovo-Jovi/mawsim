### Design specification

- States: `produce-ar` · `produce-en` · `produce-leafy-ar`
- Criteria: `UA-01` · `UA-02` · `UA-03` · `UA-04` · `UA-05` · `UA-06` · `UA-07` · `UA-08` · `UA-09` · `UA-10` · `UA-11` · `UA-12` · `UA-13` · `UA-17` · `UA-18` · `UA-19`

1. **`SeasonArc`** replaces `SeasonBar`. It is a server component in `src/modules/produce/SeasonArc.tsx`, exported from
   `index.ts`, with the props `produce`, `photo`, `locale` and `currentMonth`. The three `SeasonBar*` files are
   deleted. In order, it holds:
   - **The arc object**, with `inline-size: var(--arc-size)`, `max-inline-size: 100%` and `aspect-ratio: 1`. It holds:
     - **The ring:** an `aria-hidden` SVG in the 40-unit viewBox.
       - Its cells come from `ringCellPath`: twelve cells from 12 o'clock.
       - Its tick comes from `calendarMonthAccentTick(currentMonth)`.
       - In-season cells (`isInSeason`) take the kind colour. The others are track on the ink bed, as `SeasonRing`
         draws them (same stroke widths, same colour tokens).
       - In `rtl` the ring is mirrored with `scaleX(-1)` (§13).
       - Each cell carries `data-calendar-month` and `data-in`. The tick carries `data-arc-tick`.
     - **The twelve short names** (`monthsShort`): `aria-hidden` and upright.
       - Each one is centred on **its own cell's midpoint**, the angle the tick uses for that month, on a circle
         outside the ring.
       - They are mirrored in `rtl` by the geometry, so no text is flipped.
       - Each carries `data-arc-label`, in `--text-xs`, weight 400, `--color-ink-muted`, `white-space: nowrap`, no
         letter-spacing. This month's name is weight 600 in `--color-accent`. No label is set smaller to win a gap.
     - **The page `ProducePhoto`** as the centre disc, `priority`. It must not sit inside an `aria-hidden` element, so its
       alt text stays.
   - **The "this month" line:** the `produce.thisMonth` tag, in the look of the season bar's marker (`--color-highlight`
     with `--color-on-highlight`), then this month's full name (`months`) in `--color-accent`.
   - **The season sentence** (`seasonSentence`), as today.
   - **The text list of the season** (OD-11 §4): a visually hidden `ol` of the twelve months, each
     `{month}{stateJoin}{monthInSeason | monthOutOfSeason}`, as the season bar's `.state` spans were.

   No catalogue key is added. No interface string sits outside the catalogues.
2. **The label rule** (the arc's share of T20-a2's carry-forward).
   - Add a function to `dial-geometry.ts` that returns a month's label point at the midpoint of its cell, mirrored in
     `rtl`. Its name must avoid every forbidden bare noun.
   - `calendarMonthLabelPoint` and `YearDial` stay as they are. The guide and home dials keep their labels until T23
     reconciles §13.
3. **Sizes.** These floors are the reviewer's acceptance of §1 Imagery. Each must hold at 390 and 1280, in both
   locales, for mango and molokhia:
   - the centre disc is at least `9rem` across at 390 and at least `12rem` at 1280;
   - its share of the ring is strictly less than (`ringInnerEdgeRadius` − half the tick's stroke width) ÷
     `ringOuterEdgeRadius`, so the tick's round cap ends off the photograph;
   - the tick's inner cap clears the photo frame's outer edge, its 1px hairline included, by at least `0.1rem`. T21
     measured only to the `img`, and its cap reads as touching the hairline (`docs/reviews/T21-a2.md`);
   - the whole object, labels included, stays inside `--arc-size`;
   - a label's text box (a `Range` over its text) meets no other label, the ring's outer edge, the tick's outer cap
     (it reaches 0.8 viewBox units past the ring) or the disc;
   - every gap is at least `0.1rem`.

   The ring's share of `--arc-size`, the label radius and the disc's share of the ring are named constants in
   `dial-geometry.ts`. `SeasonArc` writes all three as custom properties on the arc root, as `YearDial` writes
   `--story-band`, so the stylesheet uses the constants the unit test checks. One ratio per width is allowed. The
   fence-auditor measured the built fonts: the binding label is Arabic أغسطس (45–47×19px) on its diagonal (225° in
   `ltr`, 135° in `rtl`). Start from a ring outer diameter of 0.68 of `--arc-size`, label centres at 0.44 of it and a disc
   share of 0.77. That gives a frame of about 9.42rem at 390 and 12.57rem at 1280, a cap clearance of about 0.12rem and
   0.16rem, the smallest Arabic ring gap about 5.9px and 15.4px, and about 3.6px inside `--arc-size` at 390. The 390 cap
   clearance is only about 0.36px above its floor, so do not raise the disc share above 0.77. At 320 the object shrinks
   to its column, so 320 is measured and logged but not asserted.
4. **Draw on entry** (§7), in CSS only, under `@media (scripting: enabled) and (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`:
   - **The cells:**
     - Each in-season cell draws once with `@keyframes draw`: `pathLength="1"`, `stroke-dashoffset` from 1 to 0.
       Before its turn it shows no kind colour, not even a round cap's dot (a dash pattern such as `1 2` avoids the
       zero-length dash); the 0ms film frame shows it.
     - The cells draw one after another, run by run in the order `seasonRuns(produce.months)` returns. Each run starts
       at its first month. So orange draws December to April.
     - The whole draw lasts `--dur-dial` with `--ease-out`.
     - Each cell's place in that order and the count are integer custom properties written by the server. No duration
       or delay goes in a `style` attribute.
   - **The tick:** after the cells, it fades in once with `@keyframes settle` (opacity only, `--dur-3`).
   - **Everywhere else** (no scripting, or reduced motion) nothing animates and the arc is drawn. The stylesheet
     also has a `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` block that sets `animation: none` on the cells and the
     tick: `tests/motion.test.ts` requires that block in every stylesheet that animates.
   - At 390 and 1280 the arc is in the first viewport, so its entry is the page load.
5. **The scroll zoom**, in **`SeasonArcMotion`**. This is the only `"use client"` file in `src/modules/produce`.
   - It scales only the centre disc's `img`, inside its circle, from 1 to `--zoom-scroll`. The scale reads 1 at scroll 0,
     so hydration shows no jump.
   - It reaches `--zoom-scroll` when the disc's bottom edge meets the viewport top, or at the page's maximum scroll,
     whichever comes first.
   - It is scrubbed by ScrollTrigger with `scrub` = `--story-scrub`, with no `pin` and no snap.
   - It reads both tokens from CSS. It works only in `gsap.matchMedia`'s `(prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`
     branch.
   - It is reverted on unmount and under reduced motion. No inline style is left after a revert.
   - There is no Lenis on this page (§10).
6. **`ProducePhoto` `page` slot.**
   - The frame becomes a circle: `aspect-ratio: 1` and `--radius-pill`. The 4:3 and 3:2 rules go.
   - `sizes` names the disc's width at each breakpoint, rounded up to a whole rem, with no `vw`.
   - Its fallback becomes the kind-colour disc with the names, the card and hero branch. The inner `SeasonRing` goes.
     `currentMonth` stays optional in the props type and is no longer read, because `ProduceCard.tsx:47-53` (not this
     task's) still passes it. The now-dead `.dial` and halo rules stay. T23 removes them and the prop.
   - The frame keeps its 1px `--color-hairline` border: `e2e/showcase.spec.ts` reads it on mango and molokhia.
7. **Page layout** (`page.module.css`). The photo slot and the bar slot become one arc slot.
   - At 390: the identity, then the arc block centred, then the storage tip.
   - From 64rem: the arc block in column 1, centred, and the identity and the tip in column 2.
   - There is no horizontal scroll at 320.

   - **sizes** (mango and molokhia; 320×700 logged, asserting only no horizontal scroll):
     `<slug> sizes disc=<rem> font=<px> minLabelGap=<px> inside=<px> tickClear=<px> labelOffset=<deg> lit=<n>`.
     - `disc` is the photo frame's outer box; `font` the labels' computed size.
     - `minLabelGap` is the smallest gap from a label's text box to another label, the ring's outer edge, the tick's
       outer cap or the disc. `inside` is the smallest gap from any part of the object to the `--arc-size` box.
     - `tickClear` runs from the tick's inner end, mapped through `getScreenCTM()` and moved inward by half the tick's
       stroke, to the photo frame's outer edge, as `e2e/year-story.spec.ts` `storySizes` maps the tick.
     - `labelOffset` is the largest angle between a label box's centre and its cell's midpoint.
     - `lit` is the count of cells whose computed stroke equals the kind token.
   - **draw:** `draw cells=<n> order=<months> tick=<opacity> again=<n>`. `again` counts the arc animations that start
     after a scroll to the end and back.
   - **zoom:** `zoom start=<scale> end=<scale> back=<scale> maxScroll=<px>`. The page may scroll only a little at 1280,
     so `end` is read at the page's maximum scroll.
   - **still:** `still scale=<scale> frames=30`: after scrolling stops and the `--story-scrub` lag has passed, the scale
     stays fixed for 30 frames.
   - **reduced:** `reduced animations=<n> offset=<max> scale=<scale>`, at the page's maximum scroll.
   - **nojs:** `nojs drawn=<bool> scale=<scale> list=<n> hiddenSlot=<bool>`. `hiddenSlot` is
     `closest("div[hidden][id^='S:']") !== null`, as in `e2e/year-story.spec.ts:215`. It is logged, not asserted (the
     layout's `<Suspense>` row from T20-a2); `drawn`, `scale` and `list` are read from the DOM either way.
   - **shift:** `shift=<value>` over the load, the draw and a scroll to the end and back.
   - **origin:** `origin=ok lenis=<bool>`.
   - **teardown:** `teardown start=<scale> end=<scale>` after the header's guide link and back.
   - **axe:** `axe=<n>` on the settled page, after the draw has finished.
