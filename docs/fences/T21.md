2. **`YearStory`** is a server component in `src/modules/home/`, exported from `index.ts`. It replaces `Hero` above the
   unchanged "in season this month" grid. It is one band with `data-texture-band="hero"`, and its `MarketTexture` is the
   band's only `aria-hidden` direct child. The band holds:
   - the `h1`: `home.headline` followed by the month slot, in the accent colour;
   - the story object, a `--dial-size` square with `max-inline-size: 100%`. It contains:
     - `YearDial`, with its twelve short month names as text (not links), at the size the orbit band leaves (computed in
       the geometry module);
     - the hero photograph as the centre disc: `ProducePhoto` `slot="hero"`, the manifest's `hero` row, `priority`, a
       circle, inside `data-hero-photo`;
     - ten produce circles in an orbit band outside the labels, one at each fixed place, in `listProduce()` order from
       12 o'clock, clockwise in `ltr` and counter-clockwise in `rtl`. Each one:
       - uses a new decorative `ProducePhoto` slot `orbit` (`alt=""`, `aria-hidden`, a small `sizes`, the kind-colour
         fallback disc);
       - carries `data-story-produce="<slug>"`.
     The disc and the orbit sit **outside** `[data-dial-turn]`, so they never turn.
   - the count slot;
   - the link: the text `home.guideLink` (unchanged), with an `href` that follows the month shown.

   The new key `home.storyCount` is exactly `In season in {month}: {count}` in English and exactly
   `في موسمه في {month}: {count}` in Arabic. The count is formatted with `Intl.NumberFormat(locale)`.

   The server renders this month settled. This month's set (from `inSeasonIn`, never typed) sits at scale 1 and
   opacity 1. Every other circle sits at `transform: scale(var(--story-back-scale))` and `--story-back-opacity`, set in
   the stylesheet, never in a `style` attribute.

   At 390 the `h1` is above the object. At 1280 the headline column is at the inline start and the object at the inline
   end. Nothing overlaps an image, and there is no horizontal scroll at 320.
3. **Two fixed slots (§1, §9).** The headline's month and the count sentence each work like this:
   - Each slot is one grid cell stacking all twelve values.
   - The value shown is opaque. The other eleven have `opacity: 0` and `aria-hidden="true"`, from the stylesheet and
     the attributes the server renders.
   - The cell reserves room for the largest value, so a change of month moves nothing.
   - The `h1`'s accessible name names exactly one month, the month shown. There is no live region.
4. **The reserved length (CSS only).**
   - Only under `@media (scripting: enabled) and (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`, the band's block size is
     `calc(100svh + 11 * var(--story-step))` and its stage is `position: sticky`.
   - The stage's block offset clears the sticky site header, using existing tokens only.
   - Otherwise the band has its natural height.
5. **`YearStoryMotion`** (`src/modules/home/YearStoryMotion.tsx`) is the only `"use client"` file in `src/modules/home`.
   - **Imports:** `react`, `next/link`, `gsap`, `gsap/ScrollTrigger`, `lenis`, its own stylesheet and types
     (`import type`) only. If Lenis's CSS is used, it is imported here only.
   - **Props:** `YearStory` passes in the per-month turn (`dialTurnDegrees`), the twelve guide `href`s and each circle's
     `SeasonMonths`.
   - **Timeline:** it registers ScrollTrigger once. Inside `gsap.context` and `gsap.matchMedia`, in the `no-preference`
     branch only, it builds one timeline of eleven steps. The timeline is scrubbed over the band with `scrub` =
     `--story-scrub` in seconds, no `pin` and no snap.
   - **Step k** shows month `((this − 1 + k) mod 12) + 1`:
     - the dial wrapper turns one `--dial-step` in `dialTurnDegrees`' direction for the locale, and the label
       wrappers counter-turn;
     - that month's set comes forward to scale 1 and opacity 1, and the rest go back to the back tokens;
     - at the step's midpoint the slot values cross-fade and swap `aria-hidden`, and the link's `href` becomes that
       month's. The link is a `next/link` with its `href` prop following the month shown, or a plain `<a>`. A click goes
       where the link says.
   - **Animated properties:** `rotation`, `scale` and `opacity` only.
   - **Lenis** (`lenis`, MIT):
     - it is created only in that branch, and one `gsap.ticker` callback drives it;
     - it is wired to `ScrollTrigger.update` and smooths the wheel only (no `syncTouch`, `anchors` or `prevent`);
     - the keyboard, the skip link and focus scroll natively (OD-11 §5).
   - **Clean-up**, on revert at any moment:
     - it kills the timeline and its ScrollTrigger;
     - it destroys Lenis, removes the ticker callback and restores any global ticker setting it changed
       (`lagSmoothing`);
     - it restores the slots' `aria-hidden` and points the link back to this month;
     - it leaves no inline style it wrote and no `lenis` class on `html`.
