   This month's set (from `inSeasonIn`) is at scale 1 and opacity 1. The rest are at the back tokens, from the stylesheet.

   **2a. Orbit circles you can see (REVISE 1).** These floors are the reviewer's acceptance of `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` §1
   Imagery and OD-11 §2:
   - A forward circle (scale 1) is at least `2.4rem` across at 390 and at least `4rem` at 1280.
   - The centre disc is at least `6rem` across at 390 and at least `10rem` at 1280.

   The geometry lives in `dial-geometry.ts`, with three levers:
   - the band share b of `--dial-size`;
   - a story-only label radius L, as a share of the story dial (the guide keeps `labelRadius` 16.875 of 40);
   - an inset δ of the orbit's centre radius, as a share of `--dial-size`.

   The fence-auditor modelled every rotation in both locales, with the built fonts. The values to start from:
   - below 64rem: b = 0.12, L = 0.40 and δ = 0.006, which give 2.40rem and 6.79rem at 390;
   - from 64rem: b = 0.15 (one ratio per width is allowed), which gives 5.10rem and 10.63rem at 1280.

   `YearDial`'s story size still follows from b (`innerDialSize`). No token is added or changed. `--dial-size` keeps its
   values.

   Nothing overlaps, at step 0 and at step 6, in both locales, at 390 and 1280. At 320 no setting holds without a token
   change, so 320 is measured and logged but not asserted; the review records it as a known limit.
   - a label's text box (a `Range` over its text) meets no other label's text box, no orbit circle, the ring's outer edge
     (the bed stroke's outer radius; the tick's cap is excluded) or the centre disc;
   - no two orbit circles meet;
   - every orbit circle stays inside the story object.

   Every gap is at least `0.1rem`. Orbit circles count as true circles (centre and radius from the box). The story's
   labels are text, not links, so their `--target-min` slots may overlap. Only the text boxes count.

   **2b. Nothing on the photograph (REVISE 2).** The centre disc's radius, as a share of the ring, is strictly less than
   (`ringInnerEdgeRadius` − half the `.tick` stroke width) ÷ `ringOuterEdgeRadius`, so `tickClear` is above 0. The tick's round cap and the ring's bed
   stroke then end outside the photograph. The guide's dial (no centre) renders exactly as today.
3. **Two fixed slots.** The month and the count slots each stack twelve values in one grid cell. The shown value is
   opaque. The other eleven have `opacity: 0` and `aria-hidden="true"`. The cell is sized to the largest value. The `h1`'s
   accessible name names only the month shown. There is no live region.
4. **The reserved length (CSS only).** Only under `@media (scripting: enabled) and (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`:
   - the band is `calc(100svh + 11 * var(--story-step))`;
   - the stage is `position: sticky` below the header (existing tokens).

   Otherwise the band has its natural height.
5. **`YearStoryMotion`** is the only `"use client"` file in `src/modules/home`. It builds:
   - one timeline of eleven steps, scrubbed over the band (`scrub` = `--story-scrub`, no `pin`, no snap), that animates
     `rotation`, `scale` and `opacity` only, with the slots and link swapping at each step's midpoint;
   - Lenis in the no-preference branch only: wheel only, driven by one `gsap.ticker` callback.

   Everything is reverted on unmount or reduced motion: no inline style left, no `lenis` class.
