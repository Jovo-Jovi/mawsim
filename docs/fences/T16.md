### Design specification

- States: `home-ar` · `home-en` · `home-scene-en`
- Criteria: `UA-01` · `UA-02` · `UA-03` · `UA-04` · `UA-05` · `UA-06` · `UA-07` · `UA-08` · `UA-09` · `UA-10` · `UA-11` · `UA-12`

`DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` §13 is the specification, its placement worded by step 6. The tree meets specifications 1 and 6.

1. **Poster** (`HeroScenePoster.tsx`, server-renderable): five stylised produce in kind-colour tokens on `fill`, `aria-hidden`,
   `focusable="false"`, no text, no `<script>`, no inline `style`. It is the whole first-HTML content of the frame.
2. **Frame** (`HeroSceneFrame.tsx`, `"use client"`): one `div[data-hero-scene]` of `--scene-size` holding the poster. The server
   HTML is the frame and the poster only. After mount, one `useEffect` asks `sceneMode` and listens only to the reduced-motion
   and width `matchMedia` changes. The width query is built from `SCENE_MIN_WIDTH_REM` (22.5, `scene-mode.ts`; no token). In
   scene mode it loads `HeroScene` via `next/dynamic` with `ssr: false`. The scene calls an `onDrawn` callback prop once, right
   after its first rendered frame. In that callback the frame sets `data-scene-drawn` on its element directly through a ref,
   not through a state update. `HeroScene.module.css` gives the poster `visibility: hidden` under that attribute: the box is
   unchanged and there is no transition. Before the first frame, in poster mode, and when the mode leaves scene, the
   attribute is absent and the poster shows. The frame is never empty, and the poster and the scene never both show.
3. **Scene** (`HeroScene.tsx`, `"use client"`), as in the tree: primitives built in code (no model, texture, `fetch` or
   `useLoader`), transparent background, `dpr` capped at 2, `frameloop="demand"`, one turn per `--scene-turn` read once from
   the computed style, a bob under 2% of the frame, and disposal on unmount. **Light:** the warm directional key light from one side
   (`--color-highlight`, as now) plus one `ambientLight` fill, its colour also read from `--color-highlight` (the same in both
   themes), at an intensity that keeps every face a darker tone of its kind colour, never near black. **Framing:** camera and
   group keep every produce wholly inside the frame, with a margin, at every angle of the turn and every point of the bob.
   **Loop:** the scene's own `requestAnimationFrame` loop is scheduled only while the turn runs. Holding the turn (the button,
   off-screen through one `IntersectionObserver`, `document.hidden`) cancels the pending frame. Resuming schedules it again
   with the first delta 0, so the angle does not jump. `data-scene-rotation` stays as the e2e reading.
4. **Control:** one `<button type="button" aria-pressed>` inside the scene, at the frame's inline-end bottom corner,
   `--target-min`, the focus ring of §9, no raw colour, present only while the scene is mounted. Its visible text and its
   accessible name are one catalogue string, `home.scenePause` ("Pause the scene" / "أوقف المشهد", as now), in every state.
   It has no visually hidden text and no `aria-label`. `aria-pressed="true"` means the turn is held, and the pressed fill
   (`--color-primary`, as now) shows it. A toggle's label must not change when its state changes (WAI-ARIA button pattern).
   `home.sceneLabel` and `home.scenePlay` are removed from both catalogues.
5. **Layout** (`Hero.module.css`; `Hero.tsx` adds `data-hero-photo` to the photograph's wrapper `div`). Below 64rem nothing
   changes: the frame sits below the dial, centred. From 64rem the hero keeps `origin/main`'s `grid-template-columns: minmax(0,
   1.15fr) minmax(0, 0.85fr)` and `column-gap: var(--space-8)`, with no third column, so the headline and the photograph keep
   their widths. The frame sits in the photograph's column directly above the photograph, one `--space-5` gap above it. Its
   inline-start edge is on the photograph's inline-start edge, so the dial (at the photograph's inline-start top corner,
   unchanged) sits just below the frame. Nothing overlaps the frame: not the photograph, the dial, the headline, the caption
   pill or the guide link. Logical properties only. Layout shift is 0 with the poster, the canvas, the drawn scene and the
   paused scene.
6. **Boundary**: no network call, no CSP change, no cookie, storage, log or telemetry; the device checks only choose poster or
   scene (OD-10 §4).
7. **Captures**: `home-scene-en` hovers the frame, not the button (the reduced-motion twin has none); the e2e proves the
   button. The report says which branch each capture took and why. Add no URL switch, flag or test hook to force the scene.
