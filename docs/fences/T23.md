This task proves version 3 as one site and fixes what the proof finds, in code only. It adds no feature, token, catalogue key or dependency. The captures are what the owner reads at G-YearTurns, so each must show the finished design with nothing covering a photograph.

1. **`e2e/year-turns.spec.ts`**: one `test` per break, named for it, over `ar` and `en` at 390×844 and 1280×900. Wait with `expect.poll` on conditions, never `waitForTimeout`; mutate nothing in the DOM. Every log line starts `year-turns <locale> <width>`.
   - **home:** scrolling through the story turns the dial, the sign mirrored by locale; after scrolling stops the dial stays fixed for 30 frames; the settled state at reduced motion and without JavaScript. Reuse `year-story.spec.ts` measurements, do not copy its assertions.
   - **guide:** the dial picks the month through its links; the cards move with layout shift 0 across a month change; the chart view has 10 rows × 12 cells and one selected column.
   - **cards:** every guide card (10) shows a circular photo inside its ring, and `document.elementsFromPoint` at the photo's centre returns the photo before any card text or badge.
   - **produce:** every one of the ten produce pages per locale draws its arc: 12 labels, one tick, lit cells equal to the month count in `content/produce.json`.
   - **bundle:** no file under `.next/static` contains `three` as a package marker (`THREE.` or `WebGLRenderer`), and no page requests a script outside the origin.
   - **axe:** `axe=<n>` is 0 on every page, settled.
2. **Carry-forward from T22-a1:** make `e2e/texture.spec.ts` wait for opacity 1 on the card names (the way `year-story.spec.ts` does) before sampling axe. Do not weaken an assertion.
3. **Updated specs.** `core-flow`, `showcase`, `texture` and the card specs pass. Change one only where version 3 changed what it asserts, and name each change old → new in the report.
4. **Fixes.** If the proof or a capture finds a defect, fix it in the module or page that owns it. Name each fix, its cause and the spec line that caught it. No fix may add a token, a catalogue key, a dependency, a route or a network call.
5. **Banner.** The produce captures show the sample-data banner twice (page and footer band). Check `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` and `src/app/[locale]/layout.tsx` for whether that is intended. If it is not, fix it in code; if it is, record the cite in the report.
6. **Documents.** `docs/STACK.md` ("Rendering" and the dependency list) must match `package.json` and the build (gsap and lenis in, three out). List every other mismatch you find in `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` in the report as a carry-forward candidate; do not edit it.
