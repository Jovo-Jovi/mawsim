### Design specification

- States: `theme-reveal-en` · `theme-reveal-ar` · `theme-reveal-guide-ar`
- Criteria: `UA-01` · `UA-02` · `UA-03` · `UA-04` · `UA-07` · `UA-10` · `UA-11` · `UA-12`

When a press changes the theme that is painted, the new theme is revealed as a circle. The circle is centred on the pressed button and grows to the viewport's farthest corner over `--dur-theme` on `--ease-in-out`. The old snapshot stays still, and the default cross-fade is off. The circle starts at the switch: the right edge in `en`, the left edge in `ar`. Without `startViewTransition`, the colours cross-fade over `--dur-2` for that one change. Under reduced motion the change is instant. Nothing moves or resizes, focus stays on the pressed button, and the filmstrip (0, 80, 160, 320 and 640 ms) shows the circle growing.

1. **Token.** Add `--dur-theme: 500ms;` to the motion block of `:root` in `src/styles/tokens.css`. Change no other value.
2. **`src/app/globals.css`.**
   - Turn off the default cross-fade: `::view-transition-old(root)` and `::view-transition-new(root)` get `animation: none` and `mix-blend-mode: normal`.
   - Add the fallback fade. While `<html>` carries `data-theme-fade`, `<html>` itself and every element under it (`*`, `::before` and `::after` included) transition `color`, `background-color`, `border-color`, `outline-color`, `fill` and `stroke` over `var(--dur-2)` on `var(--ease-in-out)`.
   - Use tokens only, no raw px (`guard:design`), and no `@keyframes`.
3. **`src/components/theme/theme-reveal.ts`**, the one module that runs the change. It exports `revealThemeChoice(choice: ThemeChoice, from: HTMLElement): void`, which works in this order:
   - It reads the painted theme before and after the change. A Light or Dark choice paints that theme; System paints `matchMedia("(prefers-color-scheme: dark)")`. When the two are equal, it calls `chooseTheme(choice)` and stops.
   - Under `matchMedia("(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)")`, it calls `chooseTheme(choice)` with no transition and no fade.
   - When `document.startViewTransition` exists, it runs `chooseTheme(choice)` inside it. On `ready`, it animates `document.documentElement` with `pseudoElement: "::view-transition-new(root)"` and `clipPath` from `circle(0px at Xpx Ypx)` to `circle(Rpx at Xpx Ypx)`:
     - X and Y are the centre of `from`'s bounding box;
     - R is the distance from that centre to the farthest viewport corner;
     - the duration and easing come from `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement)`, reading `--dur-theme` and `--ease-in-out`. No number or curve is written in TypeScript.
   - It keeps the running transition and its clip animation in module scope. A press that comes during a reveal cancels that clip animation, then calls the running transition's `skipTransition()`, which finishes it, before it starts its own.
   - It handles every promise of a transition (`ready`, `finished`, `updateCallbackDone`). A skipped transition is not an error: no unhandled rejection and no console output.
   - Without the API, it sets `data-theme-fade` on `<html>`, calls `chooseTheme(choice)`, and removes the attribute once every `CSSTransition` in `document.getAnimations()` has finished or been cancelled. It removes the attribute at once when none started. It sets no timer.
   - It never moves focus, never touches storage directly (only `chooseTheme` does), and adds no global.

   Write `theme-reveal.test.ts` beside it. Stub `document`, `window.matchMedia` and `getComputedStyle` with `vi.stubGlobal`, and mock `./theme-storage`. Name each case for the break it catches:
   - a transition starts when the painted theme does not change;
   - a transition or the fade runs under reduced motion;
   - the circle is not centred on the button, or does not reach the farthest corner (test a button near each edge);
   - the duration or easing is not the token's;
   - the animation targets something other than `::view-transition-new(root)`;
   - a second press does not skip the running transition first;
   - a second press leaves the first clip animation running;
   - a skipped transition leaves a rejection unhandled;
   - without the API, the fade attribute is not set, or is not removed.

Keep everything else, including `useSyncExternalStore`, the `applyCurrentTheme` layout effect, the markup and the CSS module. `ThemeSwitch.test.tsx` passes unchanged: it mocks `./theme-storage`, which `theme-reveal.ts` imports too.
