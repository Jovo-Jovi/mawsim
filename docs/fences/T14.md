### Design specification

- States: `card-hover-en` · `card-hover-ar` · `card-focus-ar`
- Criteria: `UA-01` · `UA-02` · `UA-03` · `UA-04` · `UA-07` · `UA-08` · `UA-10` · `UA-12` · `UA-14`

CSS only. Today `content/photos.json` is `[]`, so every card shows the **fallback** (a kind-coloured `.tile` holding the
`SeasonRing` dial and the name pill); the `.badge` ring appears only with a photograph. Both paths are styled, and the
captures prove the fallback.

1. **Triggers.** `:hover` (inside `@media (hover: hover)`) and `:focus-visible` on the card link. The same three effects
   on both.
2. **Zoom.** The photograph (`.card .photo`) or, without one, the fallback tile (`.card .tile`) scales to
   `var(--zoom-photo)` inside its frame, which already clips (`overflow: hidden`). The frame's size never changes. The
   rule lives in `ProducePhoto.module.css`, keyed to the card link's state (an element selector such as `a:hover`
   joined to the local class is allowed; `:global` is not). The hero and page slots do not zoom.
3. **Tilt.** The card rotates by `var(--tilt-card)` on top of its existing lift, and the sign flips in `rtl`
   (`:dir(rtl)`), so the two locales mirror. `transform` only, never `margin`, `top` or `width`.
4. **Glow.** A halo around the season ring fades in to `var(--glow-ring-opacity)`, `var(--glow-ring-spread)` wide, drawn
   in the card's kind colour: a pseudo-element whose static `box-shadow` is revealed by an `opacity` transition. It sits
   on the `.badge` (photo case) and on the fallback `.dial`. The fallback tile is already the kind colour, so a halo in
   that colour would be invisible there: on the fallback use `var(--color-surface)` and report it under "Design
   exceptions" in your report. The halo must be **centred on the ring in both locales**, visible in the capture and never
   extend the layout (an inset halo is acceptable where the frame clips an outer one).
5. **Timing.** `var(--dur-3)` with `var(--ease-out)` for every new transition. The only properties that animate are
   `transform` and `opacity`; the card's existing `box-shadow` transition stays as it is and nothing else is added.
6. **Reduced motion** (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, §7): no zoom, no tilt, no glow and no transition; the lift is
   removed too. Hover and focus keep only the shadow and the focus ring. The focus ring (2px `--color-primary`, 2px
   offset) is visible in every state, rotated or not, and the card's target stays at least 44px (UA-14).
7. **No motion on load.** Nothing here animates without a pointer or a key. No `@keyframes`, no `infinite`.
