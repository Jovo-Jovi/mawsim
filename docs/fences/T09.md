The home page answers one question first: what is fresh now. Arabic is judged first; English is its LTR adaptation, not a mirror. Inside the
72rem container, top to bottom: `Hero`, the "in season this month" `CardGrid`, the `SampleDataNotice`. One `h1`.

**Hero** (§6) — at 1280 the headline and the hero visual sit side by side (headline from the inline start); at 390 the headline, then the
visual, then the dial below the headline (§5). The `h1` is `home.headline` followed by the month's catalogue name in `--color-accent`
(the accent colour used as a text colour must pass §2 contrast, else use the heavier weight and the ground token §2 names). The visual is
`ProducePhoto` `slot="hero"` and `priority` (the only priority image on the site): `getHeroPhoto()` when present, else the fallback tile of
the first in-season produce row in `content/produce.json` order. The dial is a static, `aria-hidden` ring of twelve equal arcs from
12 o'clock (clockwise in `ltr`, counter-clockwise in `rtl`, as `SeasonRing`), this month's arc in `--color-highlight` with an ink
hairline where contrast is under 3:1 (as T08's dot), the in-season count as a numeral in its centre, and its text equivalent beside it
(`home.dialCount` with the count, UA-13). It overlaps the visual's inline-start edge at 1280. Size reserved (`aspect-ratio` or fixed
`block-size`) so nothing shifts. A link `home.guideLink` to `guideHref(locale, currentMonth, null)`, at least `--target-min` square.
**No animation, no transition**: T11 owns the dial's draw and every transition.

**In season this month** — an `h2` `home.inSeason`, then `CardGrid` from `@/modules/guide` with `inSeasonIn(currentMonth)`, `kind` null,
`selectedMonth` = `currentMonth`. The grid, its empty state and the cards stay as T07 built them.

Both themes: every text pair passes §2 contrast; `--color-on-primary` on `--color-primary` at least 7:1. Logical properties only; no
horizontal page scroll at 320–1280 (UA-15).
