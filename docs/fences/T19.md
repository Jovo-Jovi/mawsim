### Design specification

- States: `guide-ar` · `guide-en` · `guide-chart-ar` · `guide-chart-en` · `guide-month-focus-ar`
- Criteria: `UA-01` · `UA-02` · `UA-03` · `UA-04` · `UA-05` · `UA-06` · `UA-07` · `UA-08` · `UA-09` · `UA-10` · `UA-11` · `UA-12` · `UA-13` · `UA-14` · `UA-15` · `UA-16` · `UA-17` · `UA-18`

The guide's month row becomes the year dial; the guide gains a second view of the same data. Server-rendered, no
`"use client"`, no script: every month, kind and view change is a link and the page is complete without JavaScript.

1. **`YearDial`** (`src/modules/produce/`, exported). A square wrapper at `--dial-picker-size`. Inside it, the ring of
   twelve cells drawn from `dial-geometry.ts` (an SVG, `aria-hidden`) and twelve label slots on a circle outside the
   ring at `calendarMonthLabelPoint(m, radius, direction)`, which may exceed the ring's viewBox, so the dial uses a larger
   viewBox or percentage placement derived from the same constants. The label content comes from the caller, so
   `YearDial` knows no guide route. The selected month sits at 12 o'clock: the server render turns the ring and labels
   by `dialTurnDegrees(selected, direction)` and each label counter-rotates by the same angle, so no text is rotated,
   flipped or mirrored. The ring runs clockwise in `en` and counter-clockwise in `ar`. This month's accent tick is drawn.
   **Markup (decided):** labels are HTML elements, not SVG text. Each is centred by auto margins in the wrapper and
   moved by `transform: translate(…)` and the counter-rotation. The only inline `style` allowed is custom properties
   (`--dial-x`, `--dial-y`, `--dial-turn`) computed from the geometry module; all other values are CSS over tokens, and
   the geometry module stays the one source of angles. The ring SVG is `aria-hidden`; the labels are not, and are never
   inside an `aria-hidden` node (`aria-hidden-focus`).
2. **`MonthPicker`** (`src/modules/guide/`): `YearDial` whose labels are links. Each shows the short month name
   (`monthsShort`), `href` from `guideHref`, `data-month="N"`, `aria-current="true"` on the selected month, the visually
   hidden text `guide.thisMonth` on this month, and a target of at least `--target-min` (44px) in both directions at 390
   and 1280, no two targets overlapping (the dial's whole size at 390 is 18rem, so the label circle needs a centre radius
   of about 85–122px; the builder measures it). Two more links, previous and next month, step by one and wrap 12 ↔ 1,
   using `Icon name="arrow"` (the icon mirrors itself) and an accessible name from new catalogue strings. The selected
   link carries the primary pill of `KindChips`, grown in once. The `nav` keeps `aria-label` `guide.months`. Every link
   keeps `kind` and `view`.
3. **`GuideViewLinks`**: two links, "Cards" and "Year chart", keeping `month` and `kind`; the active one has
   `aria-current="true"` and the `KindChips` pill. **DOM order in the header: `h1`, `MonthPicker`, `GuideViewLinks`,
   `KindChips`**, so the first `a[aria-current="true"]` on the page is the selected month (the focus capture needs
   it; header links use `"page"`). Layout (§5): at 390 the dial sits under the title with the chips and view links
   below it; at 1280 the dial sits beside the title, chips and view links.
4. **View.** `guide-query.ts` gains `type GuideView = "list" | "chart"` and `parseGuideView(value)` (a repeated, empty
   or unknown value gives `list`). `guideQuery` and `guideHref` take an optional last argument `view` defaulting to
   `list`; the query order is `month`, `kind`, `view`, and `list` is never written
   (`?month=8&kind=fruit&view=chart`). `Hero.tsx` and `CardGrid.tsx` are untouched and keep their output. `KindChips`
   gains `view`; the page passes `view` to `PageShell`'s `query`, so the language link keeps it.
5. **`YearChart`** (`src/modules/guide/`): an `ol`, not a table; one row per produce (the kind filter applies, `all`
   shows the ten): the name as a link to its produce page (target ≥ `--target-min`), the counter-name with its own
   `lang`, twelve cells ordered from the inline start (January first, in `ltr` and `rtl`), in-season cells filled with
   the kind colour and the rest track, a header row of short month names (`aria-hidden`), the selected month's column
   tinted in primary with its header name bold, this month's accent tick, and the season sentence on every row
   (`seasonSentence`, unchanged) as the text equivalent. Layout (§5): at 390 each name sits above its full-width row of
   cells; at 1280 the name is a column at the inline start. At 390 no horizontal page scroll (UA-15 now means that).
   The empty state uses `CardGrid`'s copy and links and keeps `view=chart`. Rows carry no `data-reveal-item`, so the
   `Reveal` fade cannot be sampled by axe; the sample-data notice stays.
6. **Page.** `page.tsx` reads `view` (a string or an array), shows `CardGrid` or `YearChart`, and replaces
   `MonthScroller` with `MonthPicker`. The `h1` and its copy are unchanged. `MonthScroller`, its stylesheet and test are
   deleted, and `tests/motion.test.ts` follows (its `MonthScroller` expectations move to `MonthPicker`: `@keyframes
   grow` and `scale(var(--pill-grow-from))` only; reduced motion removes the pill animation). Calm motion: no loop,
   `transform` and `opacity` only, nothing new at load.
