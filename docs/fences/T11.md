### Design specification

- States: `home-motion-ar` · `home-motion-en` · `card-hover-en`
- Criteria: `UA-01` · `UA-02` · `UA-03` · `UA-04` · `UA-07` · `UA-08` · `UA-12`

Motion is CSS only and confirms state; it never decorates. It runs once, loops nowhere, and reacts to nothing but load, hover and the
current page. Every duration and easing is a token. Raw `px` and raw times are not allowed: derive 2px as `calc(var(--space-1) / 2)`, 6px as
`calc(var(--space-1) * 1.5)` and the 40ms stagger as `calc(var(--dur-1) / 3)`. Logical properties only. The settled state of every
element is **the state it has today**: an animation changes how it arrives, never where it rests, so layout shift stays 0.

1. **Dial (`SeasonDial`).** Add `pathLength="1"` to both paths of each arc, and no other markup change. In CSS, a keyframes `draw` runs each
   arc's two paths (bed and arc) from hidden to drawn through `stroke-dasharray` and `stroke-dashoffset`: this is §7's stated exception to
   "only transform and opacity". The twelve arcs start in calendar order (month 1 first, in both locales: the group's mirror in RTL then
   draws them in reading order) with a stagger from `--dur-dial`, and the largest `delay + duration` of any `draw` animation is at most
   `--dur-dial`. Use `--ease-out` and `animation-fill-mode: backwards` so the keyframes hold only before and during the run and the resting
   style is today's. `draw` may also carry `opacity` so a hidden round cap leaves no dot. Twelve `:nth-child` rules supply the delays (no
   inline `style`, no script). Then a keyframes `settle` brings the current month's arc and bed (the `currentArc` and `currentBed` classes, so
   no per-month rule) from `opacity` 0.5 to 1 over `--dur-3`, with a delay of `--dur-dial`, so it starts after every arc has drawn; this is the
   "soft glow". The numeral and caption do not animate. Write no `px` literal in the CSS, comments included (`guard:design`).
2. **Cards.** On `CardGrid`'s `.cell`, a keyframes `rise` from `opacity` 0 and a 6px block-start offset to rest, over `--dur-3` with
   `--ease-out`, and a 40ms stagger for the first eight cells only; cells 9 onward carry no animation (`animation: none`) and appear at once.
   It runs when the page loads. The animation
   sits on the cell, so the card's own `transform` is free for hover.
3. **Card hover.** On `ProduceCard`'s `.card`, under `@media (hover: hover)`: `transform: translateY(-2px)` (derived) and `--shadow-2`,
   transitioned over `--dur-2` with `--ease-out`. Moving today's `.card:hover` shadow under the media query is intended: a touch screen then keeps no
   sticky hover. `:active` has no lift and no shadow. Focus keeps today's ring. Hover shows no colour change
   beyond what exists.
4. **Chips and months.** `KindChips` `.chip` and `MonthScroller` `.link` / `.label`: the active pill's `background-color` transitions over
   `--dur-2`; link `color` over `--dur-1`. Remove `transition-property: none` from the four stylesheets that have it and set the transition
   named here (`.action` in `CardGrid` takes the `--dur-1` colour). Nothing slides or scales.
5. **Reduced motion.** In every stylesheet that animates or transitions, a `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` block sets
   `animation: none` and keeps at most a `color` or `background-color` transition (write `transition: none` where nothing is kept, never
`transition-property: none`, which Done-when 3 forbids). The dial, the cards and the card's lifted hover state then
   render at once, final. Nothing loops, autoplays or moves on scroll.

Both themes keep §2 contrast. No animated frame is the only carrier of information. The hover capture shows the card lifted with its shadow.
