### Design specification

- States: `produce-ar` · `produce-en` · `produce-leafy-ar`
- Criteria: `UA-01` · `UA-02` · `UA-03` · `UA-04` · `UA-05` · `UA-06` · `UA-07` · `UA-08` · `UA-09` · `UA-10` · `UA-11` · `UA-13` · `UA-17` · `UA-18` · `UA-19`

One produce page, read top to bottom in one column at 390; at 1280 the photo frame and the text sit side by side inside the
72rem container, the frame at the inline start. Arabic is the primary composition: `produce-ar` is the first capture to judge,
`produce-en` is its LTR adaptation, not a mirror.

Order: an `h1` with the produce's name in the page's locale, its counter-name beneath in the other language with its own `lang`
(wrapped in `<bdi>`), the kind label (catalogue text) as a quiet overline above the `h1`; the `ProducePhoto` (`slot="page"`,
`priority`; the manifest is empty today, so the designed fallback renders); the `SeasonBar`; the `StorageTipPanel`; the
`SampleDataNotice`. The `SeasonBar` is **twelve equal cells** (`DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` §1), January to December from the inline start
(so right to left in Arabic), the month name beneath each cell (English `Jan`…`Dec`; Arabic the full names), in-season cells filled
with the kind colour, the current Cairo month outlined in `--color-accent` with its label, and beneath it a text list of the season
("In season: July – October" as a sentence built from catalogue month names; a wrapped season such as orange reads December to
April). Where twelve equal cells cannot each hold their label, the cells sit in two rows of six (January–June, then July–December,
each from the inline start). The fill is never the only signal: every cell carries its state in text for assistive tech, and kind
fills carry no text. The ring in the fallback tile marks the current month with an accent tick and the page shows the same season
sentence beside it. The `StorageTipPanel` is a `--color-surface` panel with a `--color-hairline` border and `--radius-lg`, the
overline "How to keep it" (Arabic `طريقة الحفظ`), and the tip in the page's language. Spacing from the `--space-*` scale. No animation on this page; no
transition beyond link colour. Names in Arabic use IBM Plex Sans Arabic through the existing font tokens; no letter-spacing.

Both themes: kind colour on `--color-surface` is at least 3:1 for non-text; text uses `--color-ink` or `--color-ink-muted` on
`--color-surface`/`--color-bg`. Targets: nothing on this page is a control except header and footer links.
