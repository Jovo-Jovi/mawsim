### Design specification

- States: `about-ar` · `about-en`
- Criteria: `UA-01` · `UA-02` · `UA-03` · `UA-04` · `UA-05` · `UA-06` · `UA-07` · `UA-08` · `UA-09` · `UA-10` · `UA-11` · `UA-14` · `UA-15` · `UA-16` · `UA-17` · `UA-18` · `UA-20`

Arabic is judged first; English is its LTR adaptation. The page is text, and the Direction carries it through type, sand, hairlines and
space. Do not invent a card, icon, image, ring or decoration for it.

**Composition.** Inside the 72rem container, in this order:
1. the one `h1`, `about.title`, styled by the global `h1` rule;
2. four sections: What Mawsim is · What Mawsim collects · Two languages · Photographs;
3. the `SampleDataNotice` band, placed as on the guide (UA-17). The footer's notice also stays, which keeps the developer's open point
   from T09-a2.

Each section has an `h2` at `--text-xl`, weight 700, `--leading-tight`, `--tracking-display` and `text-wrap: balance`. Its text is at
`--text-base`, `--leading-body` and `--color-ink`, with a measure of at most 40rem. A 1px `--color-hairline` rule on the block-start edge,
with `--space-7` on each side, separates the sections. At 64rem and wider each section has two tracks: the heading at the inline start in
`minmax(0, 1fr)` and the text in `minmax(0, 2fr)`. Below 64rem it is one column. Use `--space-*` and logical properties only.

**Photographs (`PhotoCredits`).** The manifest is empty, so the section shows `about.photosNone` in `--color-ink-muted`: the designed
empty state (UA-16). With rows it shows a list without bullets in the manifest's order. Each credit gives the subject (weight 600), the
by-line, the source link and the licence link, with `--space-4` between credits.

**Links.** The credit links and the footer About link are `display: inline-flex; align-items: center` with
`min-block-size: var(--target-min)`, primary and underlined, with the §9 focus ring.
- Header: About is the third nav link, after Guide. It reuses the header's `.link` and `.current` classes unchanged, with
  `aria-current="page"`. The three links share one row at 390;
  at 320 they may wrap, and the page never scrolls sideways.
- Footer: the About link (`--text-sm`, at least `--target-min` wide) and the language link form one group at the inline end. The About
  link carries `aria-current="page"` on this page.

There is no animation or transition (T11). Both themes pass §2 contrast. No horizontal page scroll from 320 to 1280.
