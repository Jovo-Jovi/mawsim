1. **Every frame shows its image.** Each `img` below is from next/image: its `src` has a `url` parameter that decodes
   to the row's `file`.

     `alt=""` and no `pattern`.

   Where a row exists, no fallback tile shows. The alt text is the row's `alt_en` or `alt_ar` for the page's locale.
2. **Crop and badge** (§11, UA-19). In every frame and at both widths, the subject is whole and centred. The frames are
   card 4:3, page 4:3 at 390 and 3:2 from 40rem, and hero 4:5 at 390 and 5:4 from 40rem. The badge never covers the
   subject.
   - A correction is an `object-position` value in a token in `tokens.css` named `--photo-position-<slot>` or
     `--photo-position-<slug>`, with its row in `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` §2. The §6 `ProducePhoto` row then names the exception
     to "focal point centred".
   - It is applied in `ProducePhoto.module.css`. A slug rule goes through a `data-` attribute that `ProducePhoto`
     renders, and is scoped to the card and page slots: the hero frame is also passed a produce.
   - `object-position` is the one physical value allowed. It is not mirrored in `rtl`, because the photograph is the same
     in both directions.
   - Never an inline `style`, and never an edit to an image or the manifest.
3. **Credits.** `/en/about` and `/ar/about` list one credit per row with `credit_required: true`, by source (§6

   credit host is ever requested.
4. **Unchanged.** The hero scene, its poster and its placement; the doubled notice (CF-24); every string; every layout.
